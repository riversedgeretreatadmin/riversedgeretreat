Our Story & Mission

A Christian sanctuary built for rest, prayer, and hands-on renewal in the Welsh countryside.

Meet Deborah & Francis

Welcome to Minyrafon. We are Deborah and Francis, and our heart for this estate is simple: to create a dedicated Christian sanctuary where people can step away from the noise of the world, seek God, and find true spiritual and physical restoration.

Deborah brings a warm heart for prayer, hospitality, and retreat hosting. Her desire is to provide peaceful spaces where guests can find quiet reflection, pray alongside the river, and enjoy a restorative atmosphere of rest and fellowship.

Francis is a seasoned woodsman and craft teacher with a passion for practical creation. Grounded in the belief that working with your hands is a deeply restorative process, he leads workshops in our rustic wood workshop and helps care for our surrounding acreage and riverbank trails.

Together, our vision for Minyrafon merges faith, nature, and community. We invite you to join us for guided spiritual retreats, practical woodland workshops, or quiet days of reflection in Carmarthenshire.

The Heart of Minyrafon

Four pillars that guide our hospitality, retreats, and community life.

Prayer & Rest

Thoughtfully prepared spaces for prayer, quiet listening, and rest designed to help you pause and seek God in the peace of the countryside.

Craft & Creation

Hands-on fellowship in our wood workshop and woodland exercises, combining practical craft skills with meaningful connection.

Sacred Quiet

A natural sanctuary bordered by a rushing river, our upcoming chapel, and a theological library tailored for quiet reflection.

Christian Fellowship

Shared meals around the outdoor BBQ, communal worship, and warm, Christ-centered hospitality open to individuals and retreat groups.

Experience the Sanctuary

Whether you are seeking a weekend retreat or want to participate in an upcoming event, we look forward to welcoming you.
