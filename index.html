Welcome to River's Edge

Nestled in the lush, rolling hills of South Wales, River's Edge Retreat offers a unique blend of historic rustic charm and modern comfort. Currently offering three beautifully appointed, intimate guest rooms—with plans to expand our haven very soon—we provide an unparalleled, personalized experience. Let the sounds of nature and the warmth of our restored home restore your soul.

Rest & Restore

Our rustic rooms feature exposed beams, luxurious linens, and stunning views of the Llanwrda countryside. Wake up to a complimentary, locally-sourced Welsh breakfast designed to fuel your day of exploration.
